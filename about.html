Our story

Started with one drum roaster and a lot of tasting notes.

Bean & Batch Roastery began as a weekend hobby: roasting green beans in a converted garage and handing bags to friends. It grew into a café because people kept asking where they could get a cup, not just a bag.

Our mission

We exist to make thoughtfully sourced coffee ordinary — not a special occasion drink, but something you can rely on every day. That means paying fair prices to growers, roasting in small enough batches to stay consistent, and training our baristas properly.

How we source

We work with importers who deal directly with farms and cooperatives, prioritising traceability over the lowest price. Where we can, we buy direct. Every bag we sell lists the farm or cooperative, the region, altitude, and processing method.

The people

Meet the team

Head Roaster

Runs the roastery schedule, cups every batch, and keeps the roast logs that guide our profiles.

Café Manager

Oversees the front of house, trains new baristas, and keeps the espresso bar dialled in day to day.

Sourcing & Wholesale Lead

Builds relationships with importers and growers, and manages our wholesale and catering partnerships.

How we work

From farm to cup

We roast in batches of 12kg or less, twice a week, and log every profile so each coffee tastes the same bag after bag.

We buy from importers and growers who share farm, altitude and process details, and pay fair prices for traceable coffee.

Our cafés in Rosebank and Melville pour the same coffee we roast, served by baristas trained in-house.
